## Reconciliation and provisional profile

**Completion versus preservation:** DM02's drive to finish is constrained by DM11's actual support window and DM10's accepted baseline. A cleanup deadline is not evidence that consumers have disappeared. The provisional method records a verifiable contraction condition and keeps “prepared”, “executed” and “not yet eligible” distinct.

**Characterisation versus correctness:** DM05 preserves knowledge of historical reality; DM08/DM09 define whether it is valid. Do not silently fossilise corruption or silently clean it up without a rule. Unknown mapping is an actionable unresolved state; the required response may be a report, quarantine or owner decision according to the actual task.

**Small change versus migration ceremony:** DM06 limits DM02/DM07. A full rolling topology is useful only when supported concurrent participants or operating constraints require it. A one-shot transaction can be the proportionate answer. The invariant and evidence obligations persist in either path; no fixed batch size, checklist length or mandatory new ADR follows from the books.

**Local feedback versus real semantics:** DM04 creates a useful seam; DM01 prevents treating its substitute as proof of transactions or durable recovery. Use pure mapping tests for mapping and a relevant engine for properties it actually governs. A SQLite fixture can prove its own transition logic and cannot certify another engine's online DDL.

**Retry versus concurrency:** DM12/DM14 require durable progress/identity and explicit unknown outcomes; DM13 adds concurrent updates. “Idempotent” cannot mean merely that a script ran twice on a quiescent fixture. A restart-safe transformation must also preserve accepted writes or report a conflict under the actual support model. This is a derived hypothesis to challenge, not a universal implementation recipe.

Core responsibilities remain intent, contract reasoning, evidence integrity and bounded repair. The pack specialises a finite transition model and its discriminating cases. Schema names, mapping decisions, batch limits, deployment windows and live-operation permissions remain project-specific. No finding creates a new source of authority or a runtime dependency on these logs.
