New rules are derived from the combined reviewed evidence; they do not claim that either vendor prescribes this pack. Required constraints remain preservation, actual authority and truthful evidence. Soft defaults are conditional transition staging, resumable bounded work and explicit reconciliation. No numerical target, SQL dialect, restore promise or universal rollout sequence is embedded.

## Coverage resolution and residual limits

DMK01/06 now connect data meaning to exceptions and semantic reconciliation; business mapping still comes from accepted project/domain evidence. DMK02/04 include actual cached/dependent schema and operation-specific DDL; engine documentation must be checked again for the consumer's version. DMK03/05 distinguish mapping tests, real database semantics, interruption and concurrent writes. DMK07 gains an observed recovery counterexample and an evidence requirement, while actual restore tooling/authority remains with the project. DMK08 preserves proportionate one-shot and staged alternatives and an actionable handoff.

No live database, production workload or restore has been operated. A future local fixture cannot prove distributed consistency, online DDL latency, production-scale reconciliation or backup readiness. These are bounded project evidence needs, not reasons to manufacture specialised certainty. No unresolved general claim must become a hard pack rule to proceed to P5.

## Standards supplement and bounded impact review

The final Stage 12 audit added MS08 after the initial P4/P5 commits. It challenges DM08/DM09: an unchanged numeric token or successful parse is not enough to establish preserved meaning across supported decoders. Disposition: **qualify**; standing: **supported interoperability limitation**. When a migration crosses such a boundary, inspect the actual producer/consumer range and precision and use a discriminating accepted-value case. Do not silently stringify numbers or impose a universal money range. P5 DMC02 is the affected acceptance case; no new corpus member, runtime subsystem or unrelated fixture is required. The initial source record remains visible in Git history.
